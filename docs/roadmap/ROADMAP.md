# ALGORITHM Delivery Roadmap

## Stage 0 — Source recovery and verification
- Identify the authoritative application source repository/archive.
- Recover original source workbooks (not temporary Excel lock files).
- Inventory frameworks, routes, services, schemas, dependencies and deployment.
- Reconcile architecture and data dictionary conflicts.

## Stage 1 — Secure foundation
- Identity, tenant membership and access-control model.
- Canonical customer/business identities.
- Document and evidence storage model.
- Consent, audit trail, retention and observability.
- Tested database migrations and tenant isolation.

## Stage 2 — Internal office operations
- Customer and lead intake.
- Business and project profiles.
- Document checklist and collection.
- Consultancy cases and task/SLA workflows.
- Application tracking and bank follow-ups.
- Accounts, receipts, fees and reconciliation.

## Stage 3 — Intelligence engines
- Financial profile and credit readiness.
- Scheme and lender product catalogue.
- Evidence-backed policy versioning.
- Scheme/lender matching with explainable outcomes.
- Human-reviewed AI assistance.

## Stage 4 — Implementation and ecosystem
- Registration and compliance workflows.
- Vendor quotations, procurement and project implementation.
- Customer and partner portals.
- Notifications, after-service and growth workflows.

## Stage 5 — Integrations and scale
- Verified lender/bank/official scheme integrations.
- Consent-based data exchange where eligible and authorized.
- Analytics, monitoring, disaster recovery and performance testing.
- Security, privacy and regulatory review before production expansion.

Each stage requires acceptance criteria, test evidence, security review and explicit release approval.
